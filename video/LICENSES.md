# What the film is made of

| Part | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Narration | [Chatterbox TTS](https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox) (Resemble AI), reading with its own built-in synthetic voice. `refs/narrator.wav` is that built-in voice, generated by Chatterbox itself; no real person's voice is cloned. | MIT |
| Music | Composed for this film in `music.py`, rendered from the [YDP Grand Piano](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html) SoundFont by the FreePats project (Zenph Studios Yamaha Disklavier Pro samples for OLPC). | Composition: ours. YDP Grand Piano: [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), used as an instrument, not distributed here. |
| Sound effects | Synthesised from scratch in `sfx.py`. | Ours |
| Footage | Screen recordings of the SettleUp app running locally, made by `record.py`. No stock footage. | Ours |
| Graphics, 3D | Drawn in `graphics.html` with CSS and in `three.html` with [three.js](https://threejs.org). | Ours; three.js is MIT |
| Fonts | Google Sans (the app's font, via Google Fonts), Atkinson Hyperlegible (Braille Institute) for captions, Roboto Mono. | Google Fonts terms; SIL Open Font License 1.1; Apache 2.0 |
